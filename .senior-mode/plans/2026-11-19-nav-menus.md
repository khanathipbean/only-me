# Menus in the nav bar, instead of switchers on the page — Plan

Goal: the views of Overview and Resources are reached from the project's
menu bar, so no page carries a row of pills and any view is one hover and
one click away from anywhere.

Current vs desired (Diff):
- now: Overview and Resources are single links; their views are a row of
  pills drawn on each of the eight pages below them.
- want: those two entries open a menu listing their views. The pill rows go.

Out of scope for this round (Fence):
- The preview panel beside the menu. That needs data fetched on open, plus
  a loading state and a failed state that have not been designed. It is the
  second round, and this one has to stand on its own without it.
- The content of any of the eight pages. Only the row above them goes.
- The other six hand-rolled menus in this app. The component this adds is
  the shape they could share, but moving them is its own piece of work.
- Deleting the Resources "All" view. It leaves the menu and keeps its route
  and its code, so bringing it back is a one-line change rather than a
  rewrite.

Risks & unknowns:
- Hover is not an input every device has. On a touch screen there is none,
  so the trigger must open on tap; for a keyboard it must open on Enter and
  the arrows, close on Escape, and say `aria-expanded`. The pills had all of
  that for free by being plain links — this is the cost of the change, and
  it is the whole reason to build one component rather than six.
- A menu that opens on hover and closes the moment the pointer leaves is
  unusable: moving to the panel crosses a gap. It needs a close delay.
- `ProjectTabs` is a client component that already knows which views each
  entry stands for, from the capability work. The menu reads the same list,
  so what a reader may open cannot drift between the two.

## Steps

1. The component — files: `src/components/ui/NavMenu.tsx` — verify: the
   browser spec in step 4, including keyboard and the closing rules.
2. The bar opens menus — files: `src/components/ProjectTabs.tsx` — verify:
   the entry still lights on every one of its views, and a DEV still sees
   only what they hold.
3. The pill rows go — files: the eight pages under `src/app/projects/[id]`,
   and `OverviewTabs.tsx` / `ResourceTabs.tsx` removed — verify: `tsc`, and
   no page left importing them.
4. Tests — files: `e2e/nav-menus.spec.ts`, and the specs that reach a view
   through the pills today — verify: each new test fails when its subject is
   removed, then both suites pass.
